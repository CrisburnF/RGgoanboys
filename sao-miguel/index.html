Faith & Fellowship

Feast Day Program

Join the Goan Reading Boys community as we come together in prayer and celebration to honor St. Michael. The evening will blend spiritual devotion with our rich cultural heritage.

Holy Mass & Procession

The evening begins with the Celebration of the Holy Mass, immediately followed by a traditional procession bearing the statue of Sao Miguel.

Community Fellowship

Following the procession, we invite all families and friends to gather for an evening of warmth, unity, and shared Goan heritage.

The Feast Meal

No feast is complete without traditional Goan cuisine. Enjoy authentic, culturally rich dishes alongside traditional music and melodies.
